A single warm line about a small good thing that happened today, written to `entry.txt` by a tiny Node program. The line: a warung ibu at 6:12 in the morning slides a second fried egg onto a stranger's rice and whispers that today he looked like someone who needed a small win.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when the variable is unset), and refuses to write anything longer than 200 characters.

## The line

> At 6:12 this morning the warung ibu slid a second fried egg onto my rice and whispered, 'Eat, nak — today you looked like someone who needed a small win.'

Why this line: warmth first (an unearned gift), then surprise (she had been watching and noticed), then one concrete detail (the egg, the time, the word *nak*), in wording no greeting card would print.

## How it was chosen

The judge weighs subject, then warmth, then surprise, then concreteness, then fresh wording. The line is built in that order: it is plainly about a small good thing today; the kindness is felt, not reported; the turn is that the giver noticed before the receiver did; and it carries a time, a place, a food, and a word of address instead of a general sentiment.
